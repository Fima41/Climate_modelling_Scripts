# Global temperature trends (1880–2025)

Source: NASA GISTEMP v4. Anomalies are relative to the 1951-1980 mean.

## Key insights

- **+1.24 °C**: the last 10 years were this much warmer than 1880–1900, a common stand-in for pre-industrial levels.
- **Long-term rate:** +0.083 ± 0.008 °C per decade since 1880 (R² = 0.77, p = 1.6e-47).
- **Recent rate:** +0.208 ± 0.024 °C per decade since 1980, 2.5× the long-term rate.
- **Acceleration:** the best-fit breakpoint is **1975**. The rate went from +0.037 to +0.206 °C/decade (5.6× faster). The two-segment fit cuts squared error by 63% compared with a single line.
- **Latest 30-year rate:** +0.252 °C/decade for 1996–2025.
- **Record heat:** 9 of the 10 warmest years on record fall in the last decade. The warmest year is 2024 (+1.29 °C).
- **Fastest-warming season since 1980:** SON (+0.235 °C/decade).
